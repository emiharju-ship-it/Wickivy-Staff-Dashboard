Staff Roles & Responsibilities

Understand your core duties and standards across Discord and Minecraft.

Golden Rule Responsibility (All Staff)

Priority

Warmly acknowledge every guest within 5 minutes of entering the Discord server or joining the Minecraft server. Make sure everyone feels seen and welcomed into our cozy home.

Discord Support & Tickets

Support
  • Claim open tickets promptly and maintain an empathetic, calm tone.
  • Always ask: "Is there anything else I can help you with today?" before closing tickets.
  • Keep general chats peaceful and redirect heated debates to private messages or cozy topics.

Minecraft In-Game Support & Claims

In-Game
  • Assist new players with /spawn, land claiming tutorials, and basic commands.
  • Investigate land claim disputes or griefing reports calmly using logs.
  • Log all severe punishments with screenshots or proof in staff channels.
